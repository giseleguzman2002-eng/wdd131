Argentina

Argentina is a country located in South America, bordered by Chile to the west, Bolivia and Paraguay to the north, Brazil to the northeast, and Uruguay and the South Atlantic Ocean to the east. It is known for its rich culture, delicious cuisine, and beautiful landscapes. Some of the most popular tourist destinations in Argentina include Buenos Aires, Patagonia, and Iguazu Falls. The country has a diverse population and a vibrant history that dates back thousands of years.
